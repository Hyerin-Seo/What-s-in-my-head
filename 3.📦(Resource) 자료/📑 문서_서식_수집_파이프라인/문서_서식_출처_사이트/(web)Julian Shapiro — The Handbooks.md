Are you fundraising for one?"), 헤더 "The Handbooks Of Julian Shapiro". 중앙에 4대 핵심 핸드북 카드(HiFi Audio, Writing Well — After two million words, I've honed these writing tactics, Start a Startup — How to identify great startup ideas and acquire users, Build Muscle). 하단에 블로그 포스트 목록(Armageddon, What to do with your life, How to tell great stories 등) 및 창작자 소개(Julian.capital, Webflow 성장 총괄 이력)가 수록되어 있다.
