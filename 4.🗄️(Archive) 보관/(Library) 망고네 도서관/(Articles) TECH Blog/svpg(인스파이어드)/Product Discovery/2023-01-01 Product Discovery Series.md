# Product Discovery Series

Since we first introduced the term product discovery, SVPG has published many articles on the topic.

This is a curated list of some of the product discovery related articles we’ve published. We will continue to update this page as we publish additional articles.

### **Understanding Product Discovery**

- [**The Origin of Product Discovery**](https://www.svpg.com/the-origin-of-product-discovery/) – The historical context behind the term “product discovery.”
- [**Continuous Discovery**](https://www.svpg.com/continuous-discovery/) – The movement toward continuous discovery and continuous delivery.
- [**Discovery – Learning vs. Insights**](https://www.svpg.com/discovery-learning-vs-insights/) – The difference between learning and insights in the context of product discovery.
- [**Discovery – Problem vs. Solution**](https://www.svpg.com/discovery-problem-vs-solution/) – The importance of focusing on both the problem and the solution during product discovery.
- [**Discovery – Judgement**](https://www.svpg.com/discovery-judgement/) – The four areas where judgement is essential in product discovery.
- [**Discovery – Delivery**](https://www.svpg.com/discovery-delivery/) – Addresses the anti-pattern of separating product discovery and delivery into different teams.
- [**Discovery – Excuses**](https://www.svpg.com/discovery-excuses/) – Answers to the most common excuses teams make to avoid proper product discovery.
- [**Discovery vs. Design**](https://www.svpg.com/discovery-vs-design/) – The role of product discovery in feature teams.
- [**Discovery vs. Documentation**](https://www.svpg.com/discovery-vs-documentation/) – The argument against relying on heavy documentation without doing necessary discovery work.
- [**Product Discovery vs. Product Optimization**](https://www.svpg.com/product-discovery-vs-product-optimization/) – The difference between product discovery and product optimization techniques.
- **[Market Discovery vs. Product Discovery](https://www.svpg.com/market-discovery-vs-product-discovery/)** – The difference between market discovery and product discovery.
