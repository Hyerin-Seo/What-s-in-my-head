# Product in the AI Era

As generative AI continues to evolve, SVPG is focused on understanding its impact on product teams and practices.

Below is a curated list of our content on various aspects of AI in product development. This page will be updated regularly when SVPG publishes new content on AI related topics.

### **SVPG Articles on Generative AI**

[**A Vision For Product Teams**](https://www.svpg.com/a-vision-for-product-teams/)

Predictions on the future roles on a product team, and the team topologies of product organizations, in the era of generative AI.

[**AI Product Management**](https://www.svpg.com/ai-product-management/)

Highlights the need for product managers to understand how generative AI technology works, the range of [risks](https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/) involved, and the work required to mitigate the risks.

[**The Era of the Product Creator**](https://www.svpg.com/the-era-of-the-product-creator/)

Explores why the role of the product manager as a product creator is more necessary than ever in the era of generative AI and rapidly evolving product development.

[**Product, Design and AI**](https://www.svpg.com/product-design-and-ai/)

Defines the necessary and distinct contributions of the product management and design roles in the context of generative AI.

[**Team Autonomy and AI**](https://www.svpg.com/team-autonomy-and-ai/)

How generative AI can enable greater team autonomy in addition to accelerated discovery and delivery.

[**Tests of the Product Model**](https://www.svpg.com/tests-of-the-product-model/)

Outlines the case for adopting the [product model](https://www.svpg.com/the-product-operating-model-an-introduction/) to navigate the challenges and opportunities created by emerging technologies like generative AI.

[**AI Product Management 2 Years In**](https://www.svpg.com/ai-product-management-2-years-in/)

Reflections on the impact of generative AI on product management two years after its emergence.

[**Product Predictions 2024**](https://www.svpg.com/product-predictions-2024/)

Marty Cagan shares his insights and predictions for product management in 2024, including the role of generative AI.

[**Preparing For The Future**](https://www.svpg.com/preparing-for-the-future/)

Outlines the numerous ways the emergence of generative AI impacts how products are built.

[**INSPIRED in the Generative AI Era**](https://www.svpg.com/inspired-in-the-generative-ai-era/)

Introduces the new preface to *“*[*INSPIRED: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love*](https://www.amazon.com/INSPIRED-Create-Tech-Products-Customers/dp/1119387507)*,”* addressing the impact of generative AI on product teams.

#### **Coaching AI: The Impact on Product Teams**
