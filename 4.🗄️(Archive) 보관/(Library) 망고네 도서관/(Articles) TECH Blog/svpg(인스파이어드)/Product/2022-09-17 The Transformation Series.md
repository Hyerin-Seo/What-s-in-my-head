# The Transformation Series

We are working on the next book in the SVPG series called *[TRANSFORMED](https://www.svpg.com/TRANSFORMED)*. It addresses the very difficult but critical topic of how a company can transform from the rest to the best.

As you may know, we often [test out ideas and topics by publishing articles](https://www.svpg.com/so-you-want-to-write-a-book/), and those topics that prove useful make their way into our books.

This page is meant to list the various transformation-related articles we’ve published. We will continue to update this page as we publish additional articles.

**Transformation Fundamentals:**

[Transformation Defined](https://www.svpg.com/transformation-defined/): What does transformation really mean? What can a company that has transformed do now, that they could not do before?

[Changing How You Build](https://www.svpg.com/changing-how-you-build/): What is meant exactly by changing how you build?

[Changing How You Solve Problems](https://www.svpg.com/changing-how-you-solve-problems/): What is meant exactly by changing how you solve problems?

[Changing How You Decide Which Problems To Solve](https://www.svpg.com/changing-how-you-decide-which-problems-to-solve/): What is the role of product strategy and product leadership?

**Product Operating Model Overview:**

[Product Model Competencies](https://www.svpg.com/product-model-competencies): The four critical product model competencies.

[Product Model Concepts](https://www.svpg.com/product-model-concepts/): The five core product model concepts.

**Transformation Strategies**:

[Transformation in Action](https://www.svpg.com/transformation-in-action/): What does a product organization look like that has successfully transformed?

[Keys to Successful Transformation](https://www.svpg.com/keys-to-successful-transformation/): What are the keys to a successful transformation?

[Meaningful Transformation](https://www.svpg.com/meaningful-transformation/): How do we get started on our transformation journey?

Working With Key Constituencies:

[Pledge To Customers](https://www.svpg.com/pledge-to-customers/): How does a company’s interactions with customers need to change when moving from sales-drive feature teams to empowered product teams?

[Pledge To Stakeholders:](https://www.svpg.com/pledge-to-stakeholders/)  How does the product organization interact with key stakeholders in the product model?

[Pledge To Executives:](https://www.svpg.com/pledge-to-executives/)  How does the product organization interact with executives in the product model?

**Transformation Assistance:**

[Types of Product Coaches](https://www.svpg.com/types-of-product-coaching/): What are the different types of coaches that can help us on our transformation journey?

**Transformation Pitfalls:**

[Scaling With Process vs People](https://www.svpg.com/scaling-with-process-vs-people/): The problem with attempting to scale and transform with process instead of people.

[Process People:](https://www.svpg.com/process-people/) Be careful of the disease of process people.

**Books to Help Inspire An Organization To Transform:**
